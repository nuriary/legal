PRIVACY POLICY — Masterpiece in Motion

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Masterpiece in Motion ("the app") is designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

The app does NOT require an account and does NOT collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, photos, or location. We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect it.

HOW THE APP WORKS

To show artworks and videos, the app downloads content from our content delivery service (Supabase). Standard technical information required to deliver files over the internet — such as your device's IP address — may be processed by these hosting providers solely to serve the content and is not used by us to identify you.

PURCHASES

If the app is offered as a paid app or offers in-app purchases, all payments are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

CHILDREN

The app contains fine-art content suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

IMAGE CREDITS

Artwork images are provided by The Metropolitan Museum of Art under its Open Access (CC0) policy. The app is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Met.

CHANGES

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "last updated" date.
